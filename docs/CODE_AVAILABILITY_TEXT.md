## Recommended manuscript wording

### Short version
Analysis scripts used in this study are available at GitHub: **[GITHUB_REPOSITORY_URL]**.

### Preferred reproducibility version
The analysis scripts used for mouse phenotype processing, mouse-to-human orthology mapping, sex-stratified appendicular lean mass GWAS analysis, linkage disequilibrium analysis, and GTEx regulatory integration are available at GitHub: **[GITHUB_REPOSITORY_URL]**. Raw source datasets are not redistributed and should be obtained from the original IMPC, GWAS, 1000 Genomes, and GTEx resources.

### Where to place it
For a short conference manuscript, place a one-sentence **Code availability** statement at the end of Materials and Methods, immediately before Results. If the template does not permit a separate subsection, add the short sentence as the final sentence of the Methods section.
